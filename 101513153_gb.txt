George Brown College - Overview
===============================
George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology
located in downtown Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967 and is named
after George Brown, a Father of Confederation and founder of the Toronto
Globe newspaper.

Campuses
--------
The college operates several campuses across downtown Toronto, including
St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront. Each campus focuses on different
areas of study, such as business, technology, health sciences, and
hospitality.
