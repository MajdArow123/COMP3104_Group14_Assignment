Importance of DevOps in the SDLC

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) covers planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Traditionally these phases were handled by separate teams, which caused delays and miscommunication between developers and operations.

Faster and Safer Delivery
DevOps connects every phase of the SDLC through automation and collaboration. Continuous integration tests code as soon as it is written, and continuous delivery allows small, frequent releases instead of large risky ones, which shortens feedback loops.

Quality and Monitoring
DevOps also improves quality in the testing and maintenance phases. Automated tests reduce human error, and monitoring in production gives teams real data to fix issues quickly and plan the next iteration, making the SDLC a continuous loop.
