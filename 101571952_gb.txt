George Brown College - About My School
======================================

About George Brown College
--------------------------
George Brown College is a popular public college located in downtown Toronto. 
It opened in 1967 and was named after George Brown, an important historical 
figure in Canada who also started the Toronto Globe newspaper.

Where the Campuses Are
---------------------
The college has key campuses in Toronto, such as St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront. 
Each campus handles specific programs like business, software technology, health, 
and hospitality.

My Program (Computer Programming and Analysis - T177)
