George Brown College

George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology located in downtown Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967 and is named after George Brown, a Father of Confederation and founder of the Globe newspaper.

Campuses
The college operates three main campuses: St. James in the downtown core, Casa Loma in midtown, and Waterfront on Toronto's harbourfront. Each campus focuses on different program areas, such as business, technology, health sciences, and hospitality.

Programs
George Brown offers certificates, diplomas, advanced diplomas, degrees, and graduate certificates. The School of Computer Technology offers programs like Computer Programming and Analysis, which combines software development, databases, networking, and DevOps practices.

Industry Focus
The college is known for career-focused education with strong ties to employers. Many programs include field placements, co-op terms, or applied projects so students graduate with practical experience that matches what the job market needs.
