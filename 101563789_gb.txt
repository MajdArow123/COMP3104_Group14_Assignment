George Brown College

George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology located in downtown Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967 and is named after George Brown, a Father of Confederation and founder of the Globe newspaper.

Campuses
The college operates three main campuses: St. James in the downtown core, Casa Loma in midtown, and Waterfront on Toronto's harbourfront. Each campus focuses on different program areas, such as business, technology, health sciences, and hospitality.
