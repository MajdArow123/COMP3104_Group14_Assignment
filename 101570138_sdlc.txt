DevOps in the Software Development Life Cycle

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) includes planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance.

DevOps improves the SDLC by encouraging collaboration between development and operations teams.

Continuous integration allows developers to combine and test their code regularly, helping identify problems earlier.

Continuous delivery helps prepare software for reliable and frequent releases.

Benefits of DevOps in the SDLC

Automation reduces repetitive tasks and minimizes human errors.

Continuous testing improves software quality by identifying bugs before deployment.

Monitoring and feedback help teams detect problems and improve applications after release.

DevOps also supports faster development, better communication, and more reliable software delivery.

DevOps Throughout the Development Process

During planning, teams identify requirements and organize tasks.

During development, version control helps team members collaborate and track changes.

During testing and deployment, automated pipelines support consistent software releases.

During maintenance, monitoring and feedback help teams improve system reliability.
